# Licensing

KMPMP combines original community contributions, adapted third-party mods and
Victoria 3 material. KMPMP contributors have agreed to license their own
original contributions under the following terms:

| Original KMPMP contribution | Terms |
| --- | --- |
| Script/GUI code, repository tooling, documentation and templates | [0BSD](LICENSES/0BSD.txt) |
| Creative contributions, including original graphics, icons and localization prose | [CC0 1.0](LICENSES/CC0-1.0.txt) |

These terms permit modification, redistribution and commercial reuse of that
original work. Attribution is optional and appreciated.

## Third-party scope

Third-party and Paradox-derived material is excluded from these grants, including
modified upstream files and protected material within mixed files. Original
KMPMP changes do not relicense the underlying work; a filename or namespace does
not establish ownership.

Some third-party permissions have not been established; those components are
not claimed to be licensed or legally cleared. Attribution alone does not
establish redistribution rights. Third-party authors and projects are credited
in [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).

Distributed packages must include this file, the third-party notices and
